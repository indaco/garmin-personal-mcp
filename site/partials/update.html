how to update

Your assistant gets each new version when it starts. So restart it:

  • Claude Code: start a new session.
  • Claude Desktop: quit the client, then start it again.

A new version can add functions to your dashboard and correct problems in it. These changes also apply to your existing pages. To get them, ask your assistant to update your training dashboard. A scheduled update does this for you.

The update keeps your layout, custom widgets, languages and page settings.

The guide (opens in a new tab) tells what to do with a fixed version, and gives the terminal command.
